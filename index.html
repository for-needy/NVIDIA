Eleven Days Later, Jensen Huang Is Back at Nvidia—and Wall Street Just Erupted

Published: November 23, 2026 • 4 min read

In one of the most dramatic reversals in modern corporate history, Nvidia’s Board of Directors unanimously voted on Monday, November 23, 2026, to reinstate co-founder Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer, and board director, effective immediately.

The stunning comeback comes just eleven days after Huang abruptly stepped down on November 12. Within minutes of the announcement, Nvidia’s stock skyrocketed by more than 30%, adding roughly a trillion dollars in market value and pulling the broader U.S. stock market out of a week-long slump.

How Nvidia Reached the Brink—and Reversed Course

By November 8, Nvidia’s board announced an independent review into foreign intelligence scrutiny, clarifying there was no reason to believe national security or trade secrets had been compromised. However, sentiment inside the boardroom shifted rapidly, leading to Huang’s brief resignation on November 12, with CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO.

Without its founder, Nvidia’s stock fell toward a 52-week closing low of $125.53 by November 20. Over the following weekend, discussions with federal officials and board members resolved national security concerns, leading to Huang’s unanimous reinstatement.

Reinstatement and Market Reaction

  • Nvidia Stock: Shares surged over 30%, climbing from $125.53 to past $160.
  • Broader Indices: The Nasdaq jumped nearly 7%, and the S&P 500 rose 5%.
  • Financials: Supported by third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion (up 103% year-over-year).
